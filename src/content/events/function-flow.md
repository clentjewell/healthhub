### Function & Flow Exercise Class

My classes blend functional, full‑body movement with a strong focus on form, technique, and deep core–pelvic floor connection. You’ll move through strength, mobility, stability, and coordination work in a small, supportive group where every exercise can be tailored to your body and your stage of life. The vibe is encouraging, grounded, and energising — you leave feeling stronger, more connected, and genuinely proud of how well your body can move.
