### Relieve your pain, restore your energy and revitalise your passion for living.

Doctor of Acupuncture, Member of Chinese Medicine Board of Australia (CMBA), Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA), Australian Traditional Medicine Association.

Registered Private Health Fund Provider

Dr Mal McKay of Pottsville Acupuncture has been in clinical practice for over 30 years and graduated as a registered practitioner of Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine in 1997. He has a wealth of experience treating a great variety of conditions. There is no need to suffer when Acupuncture has such a huge scope of application. He understands how and why disease presents in the body mentally, emotionally and physically. He believes your body knows how to heal itself and it is his job to help the body correct its trajectory from a disease pathway towards a healing pathway and optimal health.

He offers private consultations in his Pottsville Acupuncture clinic next to the Health Hub.

In practice he uses traditional therapeutic methods and evidence-based practices. Including needle and needle-less acupuncture, moxa, electrode acupuncture, various styles of cupping, magnetic heat lamp, ear candling, herbal medicine, therapeutic massage, Gua sha and Tui na.  His aim is to assist those with acute and chronic health issues take control of their lives, be empowered by their choices and realise a better quality of life.

This unique and personal journey is deserving of individual care and consideration and is one you travel together through a safe and supportive environment. Resolve is gained by treating the cause and the whole person, not just the presentation of symptoms.

To aid in the continuance of quality health care in Australia, Dr Mal also lectured Traditional Chinese Medicine at University and Supervised student clinic in the 4-year Bachelor of Health Science (Acupuncture) at Endeavour College of Natural Health.

His practice is governed by safe practice legislation and quality assurance policy ensuring delivery of consistently, high professional services.
